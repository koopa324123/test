Session 26 (jetways):
- js/jetways.js (new): one boarding bridge per mapped gate. Each bridge runs from the gate toward the centre of that airport's gate cluster, where the terminal almost always sits. Tube and cab are stowed at the terminal edge, and the cab slides out to the aircraft door (about 3.5 m from the gate) when an aircraft is on that stand. Rigs farther than 2.5 km are hidden.
- js/main.js: JetwayField created with the scene; gate lists from fetchGates feed setGates; each frame, jetways.update is given the gate the aircraft is parked on (gateUnderPlane, only when on the ground and under 3 km/h).
- Approximate by design: the terminal is not modelled, so on large fields a bridge can point at the wrong concourse. Gates without OSM stand data get no bridge. Bridges are not collision-checked against aircraft.
- AI hook: update(dt, pos, terrain, gate) can be called with any gate object from the same list to extend that bridge, so AI traffic can use it once it stands on a mapped gate.
- Untested in a browser. Whether DFW's gates appear in the OSM tiles can only be checked with network access.

Each flight is a timeline: parked (600 s), pushback and taxi to the runway, takeoff roll, climb, cruise on a great circle at 10 km, descent, 8 km final approach, landing rollout, taxi to the arrival stand, parked (900 s), then removed. Flights are only built as 3D models within 100 km of the player, up to 12 at a time, nearest first. Other flights in range show as amber dots. Session 28 (taxiways, landing):
- js/taxiways.js (new): reads aeroway taxiway and taxilane LINES from the same OpenFreeMap tiles gates.js uses (centre tile plus its eight neighbours, within 6 km of the field). Joins them into a graph and routes between two ground points with Dijkstra. Routes are used only if both ends are within 150 m of the graph; otherwise the caller falls back to a straight line. Not checked against live tiles (no network in the sandbox).
- js/airtraffic.js: taxi-out and taxi-in follow the taxiway graph where data exists (taxi duration = path length at 8 m/s, 60 to 900 s). Taxi data is requested for airports within 60 km of the player. Parked flights re-plan when data arrives, until they start taxiing.
- Landing: the approach follows a 3 degree glide path to the threshold (the air leg descends on the same path), so altitude is continuous from cruise to touchdown. Checked in Node: 419 m at approach start, 7 m at the threshold, no jumps between phases.
- Roll times scale with runway length: takeoff roll 30 to 70 s (about 70 m/s), landing rollout 25 to 60 s (about 35 m/s).
- Coverage, measured against the shipped databases: 3,828 of 4,578 airports with an IATA code or "Intl" in the name have a hard runway of 4,000 ft or more. The other 750 have no such runway in js/runways-db.js, so AI flights cannot use them until the runway dataset is updated.
